# Portfolio expansion — 2026-11-10

12 original design concepts: eight Persian and four English interfaces, including Tide DEX, Cinder DAO and Kora collectibles. Each has three independently composed hero views, a 720px ribbon thumbnail and two localised 1200×630 share cards. All copy remains statically rendered; no additional client boundary or dependency was introduced.

Home keeps its approved five stacking cards. The archive has twenty cards and the ribbon has twenty-five unique items. Existing projects and images are preserved.

New pages contain a bilingual written design study and opt into indexing. The original ten image-only concepts remain noindex and outside the sitemap. No concept claims a real client, delivered product, business result or live external site.

Art: built-in ImageGen. Prompts, selected source filenames, dimensions, bilingual alt text and byte counts: `scripts/portfolio-expansion-20261110-art.json`. Reproduction: `node scripts/build-portfolio-expansion.mjs <ignored-source-map.json>`. This adds only the new batch. Native image ratios are retained, without cropping or stretching.
